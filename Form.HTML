A Message from the Lead Pastor: Rev. Juhah F. Kanmoh Sr.

Beloved in Christ, Welcome to our church family! It is with great joy and gratitude that I greet you. Whether you are visiting for the first time, considering membership, or already a committed member, know that you are not here by accident God has a purpose for your life. Our church is more than a building; it is a living, growing family of believers united by faith, love, and the Word of God. We are committed to creating a place where lives are transformed, hope is restored, and destinies are shaped through the power of Jesus Christ. As your Lead Pastor, my heart’s desire is to see every member grow spiritually, discover their God-given gifts, and walk confidently in their calling.

We believe in strong teaching, passionate worship, genuine fellowship, and impactful service to our community. Membership is not just about attending services it is about belonging, serving, and growing together. Here, you will find support in times of challenge, encouragement in times of doubt, and celebration in times of victory. I invite you to join us fully participate, connect, serve, and allow God to use you in mighty ways. Together, we will build a church that reflects God’s love and shines His light in the world. May the Lord bless you richly and guide you in every step.

With love and prayers, Lead Pastor Rev. Juhah F. Kanmoh, Sr.
